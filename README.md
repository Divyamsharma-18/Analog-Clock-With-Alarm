## 💡 What’s This Project?

Meet your *new favorite clock*.
An elegant **analog clock** that doesn’t just sit there ticking — it **wakes you up, reminds you things, and looks good doing it.**

Built for people who love:

* Functional design 🛠️

## ✨ Features You’ll Love

* 🕒 **Real-time Analog Clock** — smooth rotation, zero lag, full accuracy
* ⏰ **Set Alarms** — choose your time and let the clock alert you
* 💬 **Visual + Audio Feedback** — because what’s an alarm without drama?
* 🪶 **Minimal Yet Classy UI** — a soft modern layout that looks calm at any hour
* 📱 **Responsive Design** — works like magic on phones, tablets, and desktops
